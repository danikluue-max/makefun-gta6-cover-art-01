Vice Coast Cover Studio: turn your photos into multi-panel GTA-style key-art posters. 6 layouts, 6 colour grades including Poster Ink — a halftone comic print that makes any snapshot read as poster art — sunset-gradient title lettering, neon script subtitle, tagline strip, starburst badge and star heat-meter, per-panel drag-to-pan and wheel-to-zoom crops, and PNG export in 1920×1080, 1080×1350 and 1080×1080. Fully offline: photos are read locally and never uploaded.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — upload a photo (or use the samples), pick a layout and a grade, write your title, and export the cover in all three post sizes.
